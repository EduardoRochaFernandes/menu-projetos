third-party stock photos, videos and web fonts. They are listed here with what could and could not be verified.

**What was verified (2026-10-07):** every remote Unsplash photo ID and the Pexels video listed below was checked

## Design inspiration

`le-cercle/` is a re-creation of a layout seen on a commercial website template; all text was rewritten and no template
assets are included. If you are the original author and want this changed, please open an issue.
